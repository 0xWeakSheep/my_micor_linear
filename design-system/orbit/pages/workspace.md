# Workspace override

This page-level specification overrides the generated portfolio pattern in `MASTER.md`.

- Product pattern: dense, keyboard-first project and issue tracker.
- Shell: 248px sidebar, 48px view header, border-separated canvas, optional 312px details pane.
- Default theme: near-black neutral dark mode with a complete light mode.
- Accent: muted indigo `#7c85e7`; reserve color for status, priority, team identity, and primary actions.
- Typography: Inter/system sans, 13–14px dense desktop body, 16px minimum form text on mobile.
- Elevation: flat navigation and content surfaces; shadows only for dialogs, popovers, menus, and peek views.
- Rows: 40–44px list rows, separators and hover fill, metadata aligned to the right.
- Interaction: visible focus, mouse and keyboard parity, 150–200ms color/opacity transitions, no scale hovers.
- Responsive: sidebar drawer under 900px; details pane becomes a drawer/full page; boards remain horizontally scrollable.
- Accessibility: 4.5:1 text contrast, semantic labels, 44px touch targets on narrow screens, reduced-motion support.

Avoid portfolio grids, marketing-card layouts, heavy gradients, glass cards in the product canvas, oversized headings, rounded containers around every section, and decorative glow inside the app shell.
